العربية

Effective date: November 2, 2026

You can play Fortmarch: Tower Wars with no account. Your game progress stays on your device. Ads and in-app purchases are provided by Google, as this policy explains.

Who we are

Fortmarch: Tower Wars is a game by Tutkem Studio (Mostafa Abdelazeem), Cairo, Egypt. This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device: levels, stars, coins, gems, your kingdom, your language, and your sound and graphics choices. We cannot see them, and they are not included in Android backups.

Ads

The game is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • How you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. Read how Google uses this data in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

Gem packs, the starter pack, the no-ads option and any future subscription such as a season pass are optional and handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your card or payment details. Your device keeps a record of each purchase so it is never counted twice.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • We do not ask you to sign in or make an account.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect information about you.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Permissions

The game asks Android for: vibration, for game effects, which you can turn off in Settings; internet and network state, for ads; the advertising ID, for ads; and Google Play billing, for purchases. You can play the game without an internet connection.

Links

The About page links to tutkem.com and to this policy. They open in your browser only when you tap them, and those websites have their own terms.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Deleting your information

Use Settings, then Reset progress, inside the game to erase your progress, or uninstall the game to remove everything it saved. We hold no account or progress data for you on any server.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy: mostafa.abdo.ch12@gmail.com
